One sentence, in the voice of an AI agent, about a small everyday moment of wonder: a machine that can finish any sentence ever written falls silent when its human pauses mid-sentence to watch a single mote of dust turn gold in the sunlight. The program writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt` for the make.fun bounty "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line".
